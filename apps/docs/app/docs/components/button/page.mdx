## Loading

While `loading`, the button keeps its width, shows hopping dots, sets `aria-busy="true"` and ignores clicks. `loadingLabel` is what screen readers hear.

## Do and don't

<div className="do-dont">
  <div className="do"><strong>Do</strong><ul><li>Use short verbs: "Send report", "Call back".</li><li>Use one buddy per screen, on the main action.</li><li>Use `tomato` for destructive actions.</li></ul></div>
  <div className="dont"><strong>Don't</strong><ul><li>Put long sentences in a button; lettering is for short labels.</li><li>Pass `tilt` from `Math.random()`; use `seed`.</li><li>Use a buddy on every button.</li></ul></div>

## Accessibility

- A native `<button>`, `type="button"` by default.
- Space and Enter both show the press; the press releases on keyup, pointer up, pointer leave and blur.
- Focus shows a dashed "cut here" line with scissors; it is never removed.
- Under reduced motion, the press and release still change instantly, without the spring.
- On phones, a press taps the haptics (8 ms), always together with the visual squish.
