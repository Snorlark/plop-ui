`Toast` gets a die-cut paper edge and flat shadow like every other sticker. What shows behind a live toast is now a choice: `PlopProvider` takes `toastBackdrop` (`"solid"`, the default, a faint gradient wash; `"dots"`, a comic halftone screen over it; or `"none"`), and `Toaster` takes it as `backdrop`. With a `buddy`, the character and the toast now tilt together, so the hands grip the edge on long messages too.
