New `@plop-ui/tokens/tokens.json`: every token in the W3C Design Tokens (DTCG) format, generated from the same source as `tokens.css`, for Figma (Tokens Studio) and other design tools. It covers colors (all hex) with on-fill contrast and status aliases, type and text styles, roundness and irregular radii per corner, shadows, the focus ring, strokes, motion, and the five surfaces in light and dark.
