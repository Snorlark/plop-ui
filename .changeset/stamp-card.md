Add `StampCard`, a loyalty punch card: a row of paper slots that fill with rubber stamps. Pass `total` and `filled`, and optionally your own `stamp`; each stamp gets a seeded tilt and ink, and the newest thunks on (still under reduced motion). Screen readers hear a list like "Matcha Corner, 3 of 8 stamps". A Server Component. Also exports `STAMP_INKS`.
