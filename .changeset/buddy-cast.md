New `Buddy` and `BuddyPeek`: six characters that each stand for a state (`success`, `error`, `stale`, `congrats`, `preview`, `thinking`), standalone or peeking over any component. `Toast`, `toast()` and `Callout` take `buddy` (`true` picks the character for the kind or tone, or pass a mood). `BUDDY_CAST` lists each character's name and purpose.
