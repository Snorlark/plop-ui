Roundness: one number that makes every corner sharper or softer. Set `--plop-roundness` in CSS (default `1`, `0` is square, `2` is very round) or `<PlopProvider roundness={0.5}>` for one part of the page. The radius tokens and every component corner follow it; circles stay circles. At `1` nothing changes.
