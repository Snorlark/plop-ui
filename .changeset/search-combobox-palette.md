`SearchField` is now a full combobox: grouped results on a taped sheet (avatar, status-dot or icon leads, washi-tape meta), highlighter matches, arrow keys and Enter, Escape to clear then close, recent searches (`recentKey`), loading dots, keycap shortcuts (`shortcut="/"`), a Hum empty state, and a wobbly marker outline that boils on focus. New `CommandPalette` (⌘K / Ctrl K) built on it, and `useIsMac`.
