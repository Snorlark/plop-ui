New `WordSticker` (die-cut Sketchpop lettering with a paper border, offset fill and ink edge) and `Doodle` (star, heart, sparkle, burst). Stickers (`Button`, `ButtonLink`, `IconButton`) take `shadow`: `"soft"` (default), `"solid"`, `"stripes"`, `"outline"` or `"none"`, and `PlopProvider` takes `stickerShadow` to set it for everything inside.
