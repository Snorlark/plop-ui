# Code of conduct

PlopUI is imperfect on purpose. The people working on it shouldn't have to be perfect either, but everyone should be kind.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming to people of every background, experience level and identity.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful for the people reading them later.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews), and anywhere you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [github.com/Snorlark](https://github.com/Snorlark). Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code, temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated it is.
