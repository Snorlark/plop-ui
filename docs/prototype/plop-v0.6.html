Tokens

One cobalt marker for every line. A few spot colors picked by gut, printed underneath and slightly off. One hand-drawn font family, Sketchpop: slanted for lettering, upright for reading.

Type

Aa

Sketchpop Bold Italic for anything lettered

Sketchpop Regular, upright, for everything people actually read.

One family, 4 weights, italic and upright. Every letter has 3 hand-drawn versions that rotate, so repeats never look copy-pasted.

One inkEvery outline, letter and face is the same cobalt marker. Color is a second ink printed under it, a little off-register.
Stickers, not boxesThings you press are die-cut stickers with a paper border and a real shadow. Everything else is paper, tape, and ink.
Imperfect on purposeOutlines boil, fills print off-register, nothing sits at exactly 0°. Randomness is seeded, so it stays put.
Every press answersSquish on press, spring on release, a buzz on phones. Motion only happens when someone acts.

Callout

A strip torn off a pad. Use it for things people need to know before they act, not for decoration.

Two responders are near this pin

Mobile 2 and Mobile 5 are both within 2 km. Assign the closer one first.

Location is approximate

The caller’s GPS is accurate to about 200 m. Confirm a landmark before dispatching.

Couldn’t reach the station

Your report is saved on this phone and will send when you’re back online.
